Join now

Whether you’re a beginner, club player or pro, we would love for you to join us at the UCL Chess Society. Our membership is as diverse as the game itself, ranging across academic year, faculty and background, yet we are united by a love for the game and the community it fosters.

  1. Join our Whatsapp Community:  For info for freshers on socials, league matches, tournaments, beginner tips, and puzzles!
  2. Follow our Instagram:  For upcoming events, chess news and to view our leaderboard!
  3. Connect with us through Chess.com:  To play online games!

For the upcoming academic year 2026-27, our Whatsapp Chat will be the main hub for communication! All players are welcome, regardless of experience. We are committed to providing an atmosphere conducive to beginners and for developing an appreciation for the beauty chess offers!

Those looking to show off their skills can take part in our society leaderboard and internal tournaments (with prizes!). You can also join our competitive squads, which compete in local and national chess competitions such as the London League, the Eastman Cup and the BUCA Championship. We are very excited to meet you all at the welcome fair (date tbd)!

  • A Social membership pass is only £3, which allows you to attend Wednesday meetups at ROOM TBD 6-8PM!
  • A Competitive membership pass is priced at only £15, including all social perks, coaching from advanced members, the ability to compete for UCL in prestigious leagues, the ability to play in the annual UCL Superblitz Showdown and access to our internal leaderboard!
  • Our first social will be held during the first 2 weeks of term! (date tbd) - please join the whatsapp group chat for more info and announcements!

Looking for more information on the main events we attend? Take a look below!

  • BUCA Championship: https://www.bucachess.org.uk/
  • London League: http://londonchess.org.uk/main.php
  • World Chess League: https://worldchessleague.live/

More information

Student groups are run by UCL students and facilitated by Students’ Union UCL. The Union is an educational charity and part of how it supports the education of students at UCL is through facilitating a diverse range of opportunities for students to run, join and experience different interests, groups and activities. Each student group decides its own events, activities and views (within our risk management framework).

Student groups are intended to represent the full range of views amongst our diverse student community. The Union does not endorse, approve or speak for any student group, and their statements and activities do not represent the Union’s position.
