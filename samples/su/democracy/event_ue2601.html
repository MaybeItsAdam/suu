Union Executive: Meeting 1

Tuesday 6 October 2026
18:00 to 20:00
  • South Wing G12 Council Room

Union Executive brings elected student leaders together to vote on Union policy, discuss the Union’s direction and hold our Sabbatical Officers to account. 

All students are welcome, whether this is your first democracy event or you are already involved. Elected representatives make the formal decisions, but any student can attend and contribute.

Come along, join the conversation and see student democracy in action.
